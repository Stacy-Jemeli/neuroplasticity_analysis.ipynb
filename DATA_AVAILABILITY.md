# Data Availability and Confidentiality

## Participant data

The participant-level dataset used in this study is not included in this repository.

The dataset contains sensitive information relating to:

- Children's clinical characteristics
- Functional assessments
- Rehabilitation history
- Therapy attendance
- Caregiver characteristics
- Household and socioeconomic circumstances
- Participating rehabilitation facilities

Removing participant names alone would not eliminate the possibility of identification because combinations of clinical, demographic and facility variables may remain sensitive.

## Access restrictions

Access to the original dataset is governed by:

- Study consent conditions
- Institutional ethics approval
- Applicable data-protection requirements
- Institutional authorization
- The approved purpose of the research

The repository does not grant permission to access or reuse the original participant data.

## Public demonstration data

A synthetic dataset may be included in the future to demonstrate:

- Required column names
- Expected data types
- Outcome construction
- Preprocessing
- Model fitting
- Generation of tables and figures

Synthetic data will contain no real participant records and must not be used to verify or reproduce the reported clinical findings.

## Prohibited activities

Users must not:

- Attempt to identify study participants
- Upload confidential participant data to public repositories
- Combine study information with external sources for re-identification
- Use the model for clinical decisions without external validation
- Use predicted probabilities to deny or reduce rehabilitation

## Questions about data access

Any request for access to the original dataset must follow the applicable institutional and ethical review procedures.
